Together We Rise, Together We Shine - The Spirit of Ekatra

Ekatra is an annual event that brings together the vibrant community of students in a celebration of unity, culture, and joy. This event serves as a reminder that when we come together, we can make the world a better place to live in, by fostering harmony, understanding, and collaboration among diverse individuals. Through Ekatra, we embrace the spirit of togetherness and celebrate the richness of our traditions, while also enjoying modern-day entertainment. The event features a variety of activities, including fun-filled games that encourage teamwork, friendly competition, and physical fitness. A highlight of the occasion is the Traditional Day, where students showcase their cultural heritage by dressing in traditional attire, bringing to life the diverse customs and traditions of our country. The cultural performances add to the vibrancy of the event, with music, dance, and drama bringing energy and joy to all participants. Behind the scenes, the members of Communique play an instrumental role in making Ekatra a grand success. With their hard work, dedication, and attention to detail, they ensure that every aspect of the event runs smoothly, from organizing the activities to managing the logistics and creating an inviting atmosphere. Their collective effort transforms Ekatra into an unforgettable experience for everyone involved, making it not just a celebration but a meaningful expression of the bond that unites the community. Ekatra stands as a testament to the power of collaboration, cultural appreciation, and the joy that comes from sharing moments of happiness with others.

ECE

The Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) branch is one of the most dynamic and evolving fields of engineering. It integrates a wide range of technologies, including microelectronics, telecommunications, signal processing, and embedded systems, which form the backbone of modern-day communication networks, consumer electronics, and automation systems. ECE students are equipped with the knowledge to design, develop, and optimize electronic devices, communication systems, and software. As the world becomes increasingly connected, the demand for ECE professionals grows, creating vast opportunities for innovation in fields like Internet of Things (IoT), 5G communication, and artificial intelligence. The Communique student society plays an essential role in shaping the experience of ECE students. As an executive body, Communique organizes and manages various events, both academic and extracurricular, aimed at fostering creativity, collaboration, and leadership among students.
